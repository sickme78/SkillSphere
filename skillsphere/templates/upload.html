What happens to your file

  1. Saved to the uploads/ folder on your machine.
  2. All of its text is extracted with pypdf or python-pptx.
  3. Short documents are sent whole. Long ones are split into sections and a spread from the beginning, middle and end is sent, so the quiz covers the whole document.
  4. Gemini writes questions only from that text. If anything fails you see a clear message - unrelated demo questions are never substituted. The JavaScript demo quiz runs only when you ask for it.
